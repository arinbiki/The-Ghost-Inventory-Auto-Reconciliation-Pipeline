How It Protects Your Revenue in 3 Steps

No complex coding or manual spreadsheets. The agent acts as an autonomous digital warehouse manager operating silently while you sleep:

1

Smart Sensory Scan (2:00 AM)

Zero Storefront Interruption

During the off-peak warehouse lull, the agent scans your order velocity, yesterday's returns, and a 5% sample of slow-moving inventory. It targets only the SKUs that changed, avoiding rate-limit slowdowns.

Targeting: High-risk drift items only.
2

Autonomous Revenue Unlock

Safe Upward Adjustments

When the warehouse holds 45 physical units but your Shopify or WooCommerce store only advertises 12, the agent instantly updates your store to 45. Dormant stock is immediately made purchasable.

Result: Free organic sales unlocked.
3

Zero-Oversell Safety Net

Human-in-the-Loop Verification

If your website advertises 48 units but the warehouse only finds 8, the agent suspends execution and alerts your warehouse manager to verify the physical aisle bin. If unverified within 12h, it auto-zeroes stock to eliminate liability.

Result: Zero oversell cancellations.
